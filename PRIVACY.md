# Hold Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 7, 2026

Hold is designed as a local-first focus app. It stores working tasks on your Mac and uses Apple's iCloud/CloudKit service only to sync the current task display between your own Apple devices.

## Data We Handle

Hold may store:

- Task text that you enter into the app.
- Task hierarchy metadata, such as parent and current task pointers.
- App preferences, such as hotkey settings and onboarding state.

On Mac, task data is stored locally on your device. To keep the iPhone display in sync, Hold writes the current task pointer and display text to your private iCloud database through Apple CloudKit.

## Data Collection

The developer does not operate a backend server for Hold and does not receive, sell, rent, or share your task content. Hold does not include third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or cross-app tracking.

Apple may process iCloud, CloudKit, App Store, and crash diagnostic information according to Apple's own terms and privacy policy.

## Notifications

Hold may request notification permission so iCloud changes can refresh the current task display. These notifications are used for app functionality and are not used for advertising or tracking.
