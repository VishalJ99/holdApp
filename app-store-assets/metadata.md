- White: `#FFFFFF`

- Use `hold_logo.svg` or `hold_logo.png` on light backgrounds.
- Use `hold_logo_white.png` on dark or photographic backgrounds.
- Use `hold_inverse_avatar.png` for square app/profile slots that need an opaque brand-color background.
- Keep clear space around the logo at least equal to the height of the icon.
- Do not rotate, stretch, recolor, outline, shadow, or remove the icon from the logo.

## iOS App Store Page

Subtitle:
One task, always in sight

Promotional text:
Keep the task you are holding now visible on iPhone, in StandBy Mode, and beside your main task system.

Description:
Hold gives your current task a calm, dedicated place to live. Open it on iPhone, write what you are holding, and keep that single objective visible while you work.

It is intentionally not a full task manager. No inbox grooming, no project database, no stream of reminders. Hold is a small working-memory surface for the thing you are doing now.

On iPhone, Hold works on its own as a spare focus display for one current task. With the Mac app, it becomes a keyboard-first desktop-to-phone workflow: capture the current task quickly on Mac, break it into child tasks or sibling tasks when needed, and keep the active objective visible on iPhone or in StandBy Mode.

Features:
- One clear current-task display
- Standalone iPhone entry for the task you are holding now
- StandBy-friendly desk display
- Fast Mac capture and task switching when paired
- Lightweight parent, child, and sibling task context on Mac
- iCloud sync through your own Apple account
- No ads, tracking, or third-party analytics

Use Hold alongside Things, Todoist, Notion, Obsidian, Reminders, or paper. Your main system stays where it is. Hold just keeps the current thread in view.

Keywords:
focus,task,adhd,todo,productivity,standby,reminder,workflow,goals,notes,whiteboard

What's new:
Initial App Store release with refreshed Hold branding and icon.

Review notes:
The iOS app works standalone. If no fresh Mac heartbeat is available through the user's private iCloud container, onboarding leads to a single current-task entry field. When the Mac app is running on the same iCloud account, iOS mirrors the current task pointer and refreshes through CloudKit notifications. No reviewer account is required.

## Mac App Store Page

Subtitle:
Keyboard-first task focus

Promotional text:
Capture the task you are doing now on Mac, then keep it visible on your iPhone as a quiet focus display.

Description:
Hold is a fast, minimalist task anchor for Mac. Capture the task you are working on, switch context without opening a full task manager, and push that current objective to your iPhone as a quiet desk display.

Hold is for the moment between planning and doing. It does not replace your project system; it keeps the current thread visible enough that you can return to it after interruptions.

The Mac app gives you the keyboard-first workflow: quick capture, parent and child task structure, sibling tasks, multiple roots, menu bar access, and iCloud sync to iPhone. Use it when your main task app is too heavy for the question, "what am I doing right now?"

Features:
- Fast keyboard-first current-task capture
- Lightweight hierarchy for parent, child, and sibling tasks
- Quick switching between active roots
- Menu bar access on Mac
- iPhone companion display for the current objective
- Private iCloud sync through your Apple account
- No ads, tracking, or third-party analytics

Hold works with your existing tools. Keep planning in your system of choice; use Hold as the black-screen whiteboard for the next concrete action.

Keywords:
focus,task,productivity,menubar,hotkey,todo,workflow,goals,notes,adhd,whiteboard

What's new:
Initial Mac App Store release with refreshed Hold branding and icon.

Review notes:
Hold is a menu-bar utility app with a Spotlight-style capture window. It stores the user's task tree locally and syncs only the current task pointer through the user's private CloudKit database for the iPhone companion display. The iPhone app is useful standalone, and the Mac app is the faster companion for keyboard capture and task hierarchy.
