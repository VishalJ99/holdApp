# Hold App Store Metadata

App name: Hold

Subtitle:
One task, always visible

Description:
Hold is a minimalist focus app for people who already have a task system but still lose track of what they are doing right now.

Capture your current objective quickly on Mac, then keep that one task visible on iPhone while you work. Hold is not another full task manager, project database, or notification machine. It is a small working-memory layer for the task that needs your attention now.

Use the Mac app for fast keyboard-first capture, lightweight task hierarchy, and quick switching. Use the iPhone app as a passive focus display, especially in StandBy Mode on your desk. Both sides are intentionally constrained: one current task, clearly shown, without lists to scan or maintain.

Features:
- One-task focus display
- Fast Mac capture with keyboard shortcuts
- Lightweight parent and child task context
- iPhone display that mirrors your current objective
- iCloud sync through your own Apple account
- No ads, no tracking, no third-party analytics

Hold works alongside tools like Things, Todoist, Notion, Obsidian, Reminders, or a paper notebook. Your main system can stay your main system. Hold just keeps the current thread visible.
